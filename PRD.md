# NEXUS 2026 — WEBSITE PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

- **Product:** NEXUS 2026 — CSI Student Summit on Innovation & Technology
- **Organiser:** CSI Student Chapter, NMAMIT
- **Host Institution:** NMAM Institute of Technology, Nitte
- **Event Date:** 25 November 2026
- **Venue:** NMAM Institute of Technology (NMAMIT), Nitte, Karnataka
- **Website:** csinmamit.in
- **Primary Objective:** Present NEXUS as a premium, technology-focused student summit and provide a simple college-level registration experience.

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

Build an immersive, modern and responsive website for NEXUS 2026 that reflects the summit's identity and communicates its purpose, activities, schedule and participation details.

The website should act as:

1. The official digital brochure for NEXUS 2026.
2. An information platform for participating CSI student chapters.
3. A registration portal for colleges and their representatives.
4. A central source of event updates and contact information.

The website must maintain the visual identity of the official NEXUS poster and brochure.

Core positioning:

NEXUS — Connecting Chapters. Inspiring Innovation.

The experience should feel futuristic, professional, technology-oriented and editorial rather than like a generic event landing page.

## 4. VISUAL IDENTITY AND DESIGN SYSTEM

The uploaded NEXUS poster is the primary visual reference.

### 4.1 Design direction

The visual direction should combine:

- Futuristic technology aesthetics
- Premium editorial typography
- Dark cinematic backgrounds
- Monochromatic imagery
- Subtle neon-green accents
- High-contrast text
- Minimal but purposeful animations

Avoid generic templates, excessive gradients, colourful cards, cartoon illustrations and conventional corporate website styling.

### 4.2 Colour palette

Use the poster as the reference for the colour system.

| Element | Suggested treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Primary background | Deep black / charcoal |
| Secondary background | Dark graphite |
| Primary text | White |
| Secondary text | Muted grey |
| Accent | Futuristic emerald / neon green |
| Supporting accent | Muted silver / grey |
| Borders | Subtle dark grey |

Green should be used selectively for highlights, interactive elements and important visual details.

The website should primarily remain black, white and grey with controlled green accents.

### 4.3 Typography

Use a combination of:

- Elegant serif typography for major editorial headings, especially NEXUS.
- Clean sans-serif typography for body content, navigation and buttons.
- Large, bold, uppercase typography for important section headings.
- Clear readable text for event information.

The NEXUS title should visually resemble the typography on the official poster.

Recommended typography direction:

- Display: elegant editorial serif.
- Body: modern sans-serif.
- Labels and metadata: compact uppercase sans-serif with subtle letter spacing.

### 4.4 Imagery

Use the futuristic sculptural face / technology-inspired visual from the NEXUS poster as the main visual reference.

Image direction:

- Dark monochromatic 3D sculptures.
- Futuristic human or robotic forms.
- Technology-inspired textures.
- Emerald-green illuminated details.
- Abstract digital structures.
- High-quality, cinematic compositions.

Use the actual poster artwork wherever appropriate. Do not replace the NEXUS identity with unrelated stock photographs.

### 4.5 Animation and interaction

Recommended:

- Smooth page transitions.
- Subtle fade-in and reveal animations while scrolling.
- Slow-moving background visual elements.
- Subtle hover effects on navigation and buttons.
- Smooth scrolling between sections.
- Restrained parallax effects on hero imagery.

Animations must not interfere with readability or page performance.

Provide a reduced-motion experience for users who prefer less animation.

## 11. PAGE 7 — REGISTRATION FORM

Heading: SECURE YOUR SPOT

Purpose: Collect only the information required to register a college delegation and its optional Project Expo entry.

The registration form must be separate from the informational website pages.

It should be short, clear, mobile-friendly and easy to complete.

Avoid unnecessary questions, repeated information and long descriptive inputs.

### Registration Design

- Follow the same dark futuristic visual identity.
- Use clean form sections.
- Use conditional fields.
- Display a progress indicator.
- Provide clear field labels and validation.
- Show the five-representative limit before the form begins.
- Keep the form usable on mobile devices.

### Section 1 — College Details

Required fields:

1. College Name
2. CSI Student Chapter Name
3. CSI Chapter ID (optional)
4. City

### Section 2 — Faculty Coordinator Details

Required fields:

1. Faculty Coordinator's Name
2. Faculty Coordinator's Email ID
3. Faculty Coordinator's Contact Number

These details are collected for institutional coordination and verification.

The faculty coordinator's accompanying status must be captured separately so the system can enforce the five-person delegation limit.

Question: Is the faculty coordinator accompanying the delegation?

- Yes
- No

### Section 3 — Delegation Details

Section description:

Maximum 5 representatives per college, including the faculty coordinator if accompanying.

Question:

Total Number of Representatives

Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

Collect the following details for each student representative:

1. Full Name
2. Department
3. Year of Study (1, 2, 3 or 4)
4. Contact Number
5. Email ID

Allow the registering person to add the appropriate number of student representatives based on the total delegation size.

#### Delegation validation

- If faculty coordinator is accompanying: maximum 4 students.
- If faculty coordinator is not accompanying: maximum 5 students.
- Maximum total representatives: 5.

Do not ask for the faculty coordinator's information again in the student list.

### Section 4 — CSI Chapter Showcase

Every registered CSI student chapter participates in the Chapter Showcase.

Do not ask whether the chapter is participating.

Ask only:

Who will be presenting your CSI Chapter Showcase presentation?

- Input type: Short answer.
- Required: Yes.

Description:

Enter the name of the student representative who will deliver the five-minute chapter presentation.

### Section 5 — Student Project Expo

Question:

Will your college be participating in the Student Project Expo?

- Yes
- No

If No, skip this section.

If Yes, display the following fields.

#### Project Details

1. Project / Product Title — Required.
2. Project Category — Required.
3. Brief Project Description — Required.
4. Project Abstract (PDF Upload) — Required.

Category options:

- Sustainability & Environment
- Smart Infrastructure & Mobility
- Energy & Resource Management
- AI, IoT & Intelligent Systems
- Healthcare & Social Innovation
- Open Innovation

#### Expo Team

Team size: 2–4 members.

Allow the user to select Expo team members from the already-entered student representatives.

Do not ask for their names, emails and phone numbers again.

Validate that:

- The team contains 2–4 members.
- All selected team members belong to the registered college delegation.
- The team satisfies the college's overall five-representative limit.

#### Abstract Instructions

Display:

- Deadline: 20 November 2026.
- PDF format only.
- The abstract should explain the project, problem statement, proposed solution and theme relevance.
- Existing and newly developed projects are accepted.
- All eligible registered teams proceed to the exhibition.
- Abstracts are reviewed for theme relevance, not shortlisting.

Recommended PDF upload limit: 10 MB, subject to confirmation.

### Section 6 — Accommodation Requirements

Section description:

Participants requiring accommodation must indicate their requirements below. Accommodation is subject to availability.

Do you require accommodation?

## 19. IMPORTANT PRODUCT DECISIONS

The following decisions must be retained during development:

1. Final event date: 25 November 2026. This overrides older brochure/poster references to 29 November.
2. The website is an informational multi-page experience followed by a separate registration page.
3. The page sequence is fixed: Home → Schedule → Industry Panel → Chapter Showcase → Project Expo → Contact → Register.
4. Every registered CSI chapter participates in the Chapter Showcase. Ask only for the presentation lead's name.
5. The college-level delegation limit is five representatives, including an accompanying faculty coordinator.
6. Expo team size is 2–4 members.
7. Expo team members should be selected from registered student representatives rather than entered again.
8. The Expo abstract deadline is 20 November 2026.
9. Accommodation requests are subject to availability.
10. Jury management and project evaluation are deferred to a later phase.
11. Keep registration short and collect only essential information.
12. The website's visual identity must remain consistent with the supplied NEXUS poster and brochure.

## FINAL PRODUCT DIRECTION

NEXUS should feel like a flagship technology summit with its own distinct identity — not a generic college event website.

The public website should communicate the event, its hosts, schedule and opportunities through a visually engaging experience. The registration system should be straightforward enough for a college representative to complete without unnecessary effort.

One identity. One connected student community. One day of innovation.
